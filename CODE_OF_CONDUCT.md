# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of Bible Pro pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community focused on our shared goal: making biblical scholarship accessible to all.

## Our Standards

### Examples of Behavior that Contributes to a Positive Environment

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, experiences, and theological perspectives
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community and project
- Showing patience with those learning or asking questions
- Celebrating the contributions of others
- Maintaining professional discourse even in disagreement

### Examples of Unacceptable Behavior

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Proselytizing or attempting to convert others to a particular faith or denomination
- Engaging in theological debates that are off-topic or disruptive to the project
- Dismissing or attacking members based on their religious beliefs or lack thereof
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Special Considerations for a Religious Text Platform

### Theological Diversity

Bible Pro serves users from many faith traditions, denominations, and perspectives. Contributors and users should:

- **Respect theological diversity**: Present data objectively without favoring particular interpretations
- **Avoid denominational bias**: Code, documentation, and data should not promote one tradition over others
- **Focus on scholarship**: Discussions should center on textual, historical, and linguistic matters
- **Separate belief from contribution**: Your personal theological views should not affect code quality or data integrity

### Religious Respect

While Bible Pro focuses on biblical texts, we respect all people:

- **Welcome non-believers**: Atheists, agnostics, and those of other faiths are welcome contributors
- **No evangelizing**: This is a technical project, not a missionary platform
- **Critique ideas, not faith**: Discuss methods and approaches, not personal beliefs
- **Assume good faith**: Recognize that people approach this project from different motivations

### Data Integrity

- Present multiple scholarly perspectives fairly
- Cite sources accurately
- Don't hide data that challenges particular views
- Maintain academic rigor over theological preference

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories (code, issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Communication channels (email, Discord, forums)
- Community events (virtual or in-person)
- Social media when representing the project

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

### Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at:

**conduct@biblepro.app**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### What to Include

- Your contact information
- Names (usernames) of individuals involved
- Description of the behavior
- When and where it occurred
- Any additional context or evidence
- Whether you've already tried to address it

### Confidentiality

- Reports will be kept confidential
- Only enforcement team will have access
- We will not discuss reports publicly
- Reporter identity protected unless required by law

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines in determining consequences:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

## Appeals

If you believe you have been falsely or unfairly accused of violating this Code of Conduct, you should notify conduct@biblepro.app with a concise description of your grievance. Appeals will be reviewed by members of the enforcement team who were not involved in the original decision.
